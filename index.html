Case #1 · Digital Growth & UX

+157% Peak Uplift: High-Intent Channel CRO — WhatsApp Widget Redesign

After an all-time record Form CVR in October, I pivoted to the WhatsApp CTA widget — the primary source for qualified Show meetings — reversing the historical December decline.

+157.0%Peak WhatsApp CVR Uplift
RoleDigital Growth & UX Optimization Analyst
TimeframeDec 2025 – Apr 2026
FocusWhatsApp CTA widget (high-intent channel)
Funnel stageSAL → SHOW
Context Approach Execution Results → Skills
01 · ContextProcess: Observe · Research

The situation

After driving an all-time record Form CVR in October (+40.6%), I anticipated the historical December drop in form submissions and needed a way to protect Show-meeting volume through the seasonal decline.

02 · ApproachProcess: Analyze · Prioritize

The approach

Rather than keep squeezing an already-saturated form channel, I strategically pivoted to optimize the WhatsApp CTA widget — the primary marketing source for qualified Show meetings.

03 · ExecutionProcess: Experiment

What I shipped

  • 1Upgraded the widget with expanded pill triggers instead of a static icon.
  • 2Wrote dynamic, contextual CTA copy for the trigger.
  • 3Added urgency badges to draw attention without feeling intrusive.
04 · Results & Key MetricsProcess: Measure

What the data showed

+109.3%CVR uplift, Dec pivot
+157.0%CVR uplift, peak (Apr)
ReversedSeasonal Dec decline

Business impact: protected the team's annual targets by unlocking a second high-intent channel exactly when the primary form channel was seasonally declining.

05 · Skills & TakeawaysProcess: Learn · Scale

What stayed with me

Conversion Rate OptimizationLead Generation
Channel diversification is a CRO lever, not just a marketing one.
When your best channel saturates, unlocking a second one can beat squeezing the first harder.
Next: apply the same expand-with-trigger pattern to other conversational entry points.
